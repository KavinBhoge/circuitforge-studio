Shortcuts & tips

  

    
Circuit: click pin → click pin = wire • click empty canvas while wiring = bend point • double-click wire = add bend • drag bends to reshape • R rotate • Del delete • Ctrl+D duplicate • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo / redo • Space start/stop simulation • 1-4 switch views

    
Simulation: press buttons, turn knobs, drag the ultrasonic target, click PIR/IR/RFID parts, open a sensor's popover (click it while running) for sliders. The microphone module listens to your device mic, the LDR can use your camera brightness, GPS can use your location, MPU6050 can use device tilt.

    
PCB: drag footprints • R rotate • F flip side • Draw trace tool: click a pad, click to add corners, click a pad of the same net to finish, V changes layer (adds a via), Esc cancels • Del removes the selected trace.
